Our rates

Rocapulco Supreme is licensed by the NYC Taxi & Limousine Commission, TLC Base # B00639. These are the only rates we charge. Every ride gets an accurate, binding price quote before you book (35 RCNY §59B-23), and the price never goes up after you book unless you change the pickup, destination, stops or vehicle type.

What is never charged

  • No surge, peak or demand pricing. No price multipliers, ever.
  • No booking, service, cancellation, airport or wheelchair-accessible (WAV) fees. A WAV ride costs the same as a Sedan.

Complaints

Report a problem from your receipt, call dispatch at (718) 474-7100, or contact the TLC by calling 311 or at portal.311.nyc.gov. Only the TLC can suspend or revoke a driver's TLC license.
