Privacy policy

Version 2026-10-22b

What we collect

  • Guest riders: the name and phone you type, saved only in your browser.
  • Account riders: email or Google sign-in, name, phone, and your bookings, stored with our server provider (Supabase, US-East).
  • During a ride: pickup, drop-off, note for the driver, fare and payment method; the driver's live location while on your trip.
  • Problem reports you choose to send.

Who sees it

  • The driver who accepts your ride sees your first name, the last 4 digits of your phone, pickup, drop-off and note. Drivers who decline see no name or phone.
  • Rocapulco dispatchers see bookings so they can help you.
  • We never sell your data and run no ads or ad trackers.

Payment

The app never takes or stores card numbers. You pay your driver directly.

How long we keep it

Device data stays until you delete it. Ride records (bookings, ride events such as accept, pickup and drop-off, and problem reports) are kept for as long as NYC TLC rules require a for-hire base to keep trip records, then deleted.

Driver applications

If you apply to drive, we keep your name, phone, email, license and vehicle details, and the document photos you upload, so dispatch can review them. Only Rocapulco dispatchers can see them. Call dispatch to have an application deleted.

Your choices

  • Delete my data (Wallet screen) erases everything stored in this browser immediately.
  • To delete server data, call dispatch at (718) 474-7100.
