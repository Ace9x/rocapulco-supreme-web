Privacy policy

Version 2026-10-28

What we collect

  • Guest riders: the name and phone you type, saved only in your browser.
  • Account riders: email or Google sign-in, name, phone, and your bookings, stored with our server provider (Supabase, US-East).
  • During a ride: pickup, drop-off, note for the driver, fare and payment method; the driver's live location while on your trip.
  • Problem reports you choose to send.

Face ID, Touch ID and passkeys

You can add a passkey to your account and sign in with Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello, your phone screen lock or a security key. We never collect, receive or store your face, fingerprint or any other biometric data. The scan happens only on your device, which keeps the private key; we store only a public key, a credential ID, a sign-in counter, the passkey name and the dates it was added and last used. Remove a passkey anytime in your account settings, and it is deleted when you delete your account. Passkeys are shared across our apps on ace9x.github.io that use the same account.

Who sees it

  • The driver who accepts your ride sees your first name, the last 4 digits of your phone, pickup, drop-off and note. Drivers who decline see no name or phone.
  • Rocapulco dispatchers see bookings so they can help you.
  • We never sell your data and run no ads or ad trackers.

Payment

The app never takes or stores card numbers. You pay your driver directly.

How long we keep it

Device data stays until you delete it. Ride records (bookings, ride events such as accept, pickup and drop-off, and problem reports) are kept for 19 months, then deleted automatically. NYC TLC rules require a for-hire base to keep trip records for 18 months (35 RCNY §59B-19(b)(2)); we add one month as a safety margin. Records tied to an open complaint are kept until it is resolved.

Driver applications

If you apply to drive, we keep your name, phone, email, license and vehicle details, and the document photos you upload, so dispatch can review them. Only Rocapulco dispatchers can see them. Call dispatch to have an application deleted.

Location

  • Riders: we ask before using your location. It sets your pickup and shows nearby cars, and is sent to your driver only during your ride. You can always type an address instead.
  • Drivers: location is shared only while you are online or on a trip, after you agree on the Go online screen. Going offline stops tracking; we send one "offline" notice with no position.

How we protect it (NY SHIELD Act)

We keep a written security program for the personal data we hold: data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS); the database enforces row-level security so riders see only their own bookings and only Rocapulco dispatchers can see bookings, driver documents and applications; ride-changing messages are digitally signed so they can't be forged; staff access is invite-only and can be revoked at once; and we review access and these controls regularly. If a breach exposes your private information, we will notify you, and the New York Attorney General and other agencies as the law requires, without unreasonable delay.

Getting a copy of your data

Your ride history and receipts are on the Wallet screen; use Share/Copy on any receipt. To get a copy of everything we hold on the server about your phone number, call dispatch or email us and we will send it within 45 days.

Cookies & storage

We use no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics. The app stores your settings, saved places and ride history in your browser's local storage on this device, and a sign-in token if you have a staff account. Delete my data clears it.
