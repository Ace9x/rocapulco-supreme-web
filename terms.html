Terms & fare policy

Version 2026-10-28

Fares

Full rate table: rates.html.

  • Black Sedan: $3.50 base + $2.50/mile + $0.40/minute, $10 minimum. JFK $75 flat, LaGuardia $82.50 flat.
  • Black SUV: 1.5× every Sedan rate ($15 minimum, JFK $112.50, LaGuardia $123.75).
  • Locked fare: the price shown when you book is the price you pay. Traffic or a slower route never changes it. It changes only if you edit the drop-off, and you see and confirm the new price first.
  • No surge, ever. No booking or service fees.
  • Tolls are not included and are paid to the driver at cost. Tips are optional.

Cancellations & no-shows

  • Cancelling is free for 3 minutes after a driver accepts, and always free if the driver is more than 5 minutes later than promised.
  • There are no cancellation fees; late cancels are noted for drivers.
  • If you are not at the pickup, the driver may cancel as a rider no-show. You are not charged.
  • If a driver doesn't show within 5 minutes of the promised time, you can report it and cancel at no charge.
  • If a driver cancels, you are never charged.

Payment

You pay the driver directly by cash, card reader, Zelle or Venmo, once, for the amount shown. Never pay twice. Report any request for more using Report a problem on your receipt.

Service

Rides are provided by independent licensed drivers dispatched by Rocapulco Supreme. Demo mode shows clearly labeled simulated drivers for testing only.

Who provides your ride

Rides are dispatched by Rocapulco Supreme, a NYC TLC-licensed for-hire base (TLC base license # B00639), and driven by independent TLC-licensed drivers. Your driver's name, car, plate and TLC license numbers are shown before pickup and on your receipt.

Complaints

Tell us first: Report a problem on your receipt gives you a ticket number, or call dispatch at (718) 474-7100. You can also file a complaint about any for-hire ride with the NYC Taxi & Limousine Commission through NYC 311 (call 311 or 212-639-9675 from outside NYC). Have the date, time, pickup, plate and base name ready; they are on your receipt.

Your price quote

When you enter your destination you get an accurate, binding price quote before you book (35 RCNY §59B-23). We honor it unless you change the pickup date, time or location, the destination or number of stops, or the vehicle type. You can always ask dispatch for a fare estimate by phone.

Accessibility & wheelchair-accessible rides

Need a wheelchair-accessible vehicle (WAV)? Tick I need a wheelchair-accessible vehicle when booking or call dispatch at (718) 474-7100. A WAV ride costs the same as a Sedan ride. Service animals and folding mobility aids always ride free; drivers may not refuse them. If we can't find a WAV quickly, dispatch will tell you and help arrange one. Trouble using this site with a screen reader or keyboard? Call dispatch and we will book for you.

Riders

You must be 18 or older to book. Children under 18 must ride with an adult. Be at the pickup on time; your driver waits up to 5 minutes after arriving.

Fare disputes

If you think you were charged more than the locked price, report it within 30 days. Dispatch checks the trip record and, if you were overcharged, arranges a refund of the difference.
