Drive with Rocapulco

A Queens for-hire base that knows its drivers by name. Keep 70% of every fare, get rides from dispatch and the Rocapulco app, and drive your own schedule.

Apply now

Driving for Rocapulco is by invitation. Applying is free.

Why drivers choose us

  • You keep 70% of each fare — the base keeps 30%.
  • Upfront fares riders already agreed to: Sedan from $10, JFK $75, LaGuardia $82.50 (SUV 1.5×).
  • A real dispatcher on the phone, not just an app.
  • Riders pay you directly — cash, card, Zelle or Venmo.
  • Set your own hours. Go online when you want.

What you need

  • NY DMV driver license
  • TLC Driver License (or apply for one — we can point you to the steps)
  • TLC-licensed for-hire vehicle (Sedan, SUV or wheelchair-accessible)
  • Vehicle registration and TLC-compliant insurance

How it works

  1. Apply online in 3 steps (about 10 minutes). Your progress saves as you go.
  2. Upload clear photos of your documents.
  3. Dispatch reviews and calls you — usually within 2 business days.
  4. Approved drivers get a private invite link to the Rocapulco Driver app.
